What Riddim Yard is

One street. Two sounds. One crowd decide.

Twenty four boxes a side

We started in a car park in 2019 with one Saturday and two borrowed sets. Now the block fills up by ten.

No judges in a suit. The horns blow, the lighters go up and the meter on the wall tells the truth.

Free before ten

J$500 after. Children under twelve with a big person, free all night.

Rules of the clash

Painted on the wall by the gate. Read them before you start a argument.

  1. 1

    Four rounds. Warm up, juggling, dub fi dub, then tune fi tune for the finale.

  2. 2

    Dub plates only from round three. If your sound name is not in the song, it does not count.

  3. 3

    Ten minutes a round, fifteen for the finale. Run over and the MC pull the plug. No argument.

  4. 4

    The crowd is the judge. Horns, lighters and the meter on the wall. No paper scores.

  5. 5

    No bottles, no fighting. Bad vibes get walked out the gate and do not come back.

  6. 6

    Winner keeps the Yard Cup for a month and opens the next clash.

Questions

Ask the MC, ask the man at the gate, or read these first. Most answers are here.

1Is it safe to come downtown at night?

The block is closed off with our own stewards at both ends and the police post is two streets away. Come with friends, park at the taxi drop or get dropped off, and you will be fine.

2How loud is it really?

Loud enough to feel it in your chest. We sell foam earplugs at the gate for a hundred dollars, and the space by the food row is quieter.

3Can I bring my own sound to clash?

Come to a night, introduce yourself to the MC and bring a mix with at least ten specials in your name. New sounds get a warm up slot first.

4What if it rain?

The boxes go under tarpaulin and we wait it out. If the forecast is bad all night, we move to the Sunday and post it here by Friday.

5Do I need cash?

Bring cash for the food row and the gate after ten. Some vendors take bank transfer, but the signal on the street is not always nice.
